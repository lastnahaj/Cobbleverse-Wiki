Cobbleverse uses **FLAN** for land protection. The public reference currently confirms two ways to think about claiming: a golden hoe is the claim tool, and a Chunk Anchor represents a single-chunk claim concept.

## Before you claim

Choose land with enough room for your build and be considerate of nearby players. Exact separation requirements have not been supplied, so the wiki does not publish a number.

## Claim tool

The confirmed claim tool is a **golden hoe**. The exact interaction sequence and any required permissions are awaiting staff verification.

## Chunk Anchors

Cobbleverse uses a Chunk Anchor concept for single-chunk claiming. Placement behavior, acquisition details, and limits are intentionally not documented until authoritative configuration is available.

> **Verification note:** Claim limits, donor amounts, permission nodes, and staff exceptions are not currently published here.

## Troubleshooting checklist

- Confirm you are using the intended claim tool.
- Check whether the chunk overlaps an existing protected area.
- Keep the exact location handy when asking staff for help.
- Use the official Discord for current support guidance.
