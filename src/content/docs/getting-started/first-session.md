## First-session order

1. Review every starter tab before confirming; the selection prompt is configured for one choice.
2. Inspect the supplied hotbar items and keep the capture supplies accessible.
3. Catch one nearby, manageable wild Pokémon without spending every starting Poké Ball.
4. Mark a useful base, village, or service location on the minimap.
5. Learn healing and storage before taking a long route.
6. Open the Trainer Card and follow its active Kanto checkpoint.

There is no verified early-game fast-travel item in this route. Travel on foot, keep the minimap legible, and treat a ride-capable Pokémon as a later convenience rather than an opening requirement.

## Starter choices and supplies

The first-join menu includes the standard regional groups from Kanto through Paldea, plus three custom groups:

| Group   | Choices                   |
| ------- | ------------------------- |
| Pallet  | Pichu, Pikachu, Eevee     |
| Lumya   | Budew, Bagon, Azurill     |
| Cosplay | Costumed Pikachu variants |

After confirmation, the level 5 starter joins the party. In the documented 1.7.42 profile the starter pack contains an Iron Backpack, red Pokédex, 10 Poké Balls, 10 Oran Berries, a Trainer Card, and the Cobbleverse guidebook.

There is no universal best starter. Choose for the route you want, then use nearby catches to cover weaknesses.

## Catch, heal, and store

Challenge a nearby wild Pokémon at a manageable level, weaken it only when safe, and choose **Catch**. Leave enough Poké Balls to abandon an unlucky encounter and try another target.

Use a village Pokémon Center or the available healing service before a longer route. The documented default binding opens Pokémon storage with **P**; if it does not, check Minecraft Controls for a local conflict. The in-game Box is different from any browser-based planning tool.

CobbleDollars are an account balance, not an inventory stack. Merchant screens show the bank value. A fresh profile may begin at zero, while configured NPC and wild-Pokémon victories can supply early income.

## First Kanto objective

The documented Trainer Card starts in Kanto with Brock. It shows a current cap of 25 and a cap of 30 after victory. Use the card as the authority when a hosted server differs from the static reference.

Before looking for Brock:

- keep at least two usable party members;
- preserve capture and healing supplies;
- obtain a Kanto Gym map from a village Map Guide or a compatible regional Cartography Table;
- train within the active cap; and
- prepare actual Water, Grass, or Fighting coverage instead of relying on type labels alone.
