## Before entering

- Match the exact structure, region, and prerequisite shown by the active route.
- Bring healing, capture supplies, food, and enough empty inventory space for route items.
- Mark the entrance and any vertical transition on the minimap.
- In shared or multi-entrance structures, follow the documented trainer order rather than assuming the nearest NPC is first.
- Do not treat a located structure as proof that every trainer or chest is unlocked for the current progression state.

## Recovery checks

If a trainer is missing or refuses battle, confirm the previous visible checkpoint and the active Trainer Card series. If a chest is empty on multiplayer, record the location privately and ask server staff about shared-loot or regeneration rules before resetting anything.
