Many Cobbleverse structures are tied to the trainer series currently active in the public base pack. After defeating a champion, restart a private world or server once so the next region's generation rules load before exploring new chunks.

## Kanto

- Articuno Tower — Snowy Plains
- Zapdos Tower — Stony Shore
- Moltres Tower — Nether Wastes
- Team Rocket Tower — Badlands; the route supplies the Ominous Key and Mewtwo materials
- Ash's House — Plains
- Mew Temple — Jungle; its altar is used after Champion Blue's route
- Kanto League / Elite Four Tower — The End

## Johto

- Bell Tower — Oak Forest; Ho-Oh route
- Burned Tower — near Birch Forest; legendary beasts and Rainbow Wing route
- Whirl Island — Deep Cold Ocean; Lugia route
- Rocket Radio Tower — Savanna; Silver Wing and Rocket storyline
- Ilex Shrine — Taiga; Celebi route

## Hoenn

- Regice Temple — Snowy Badlands
- Regirock Temple — Lush Desert
- Registeel Temple — Alpine Highlands
- Kyogre Temple — Deep Cold Ocean
- Groudon Volcano — Deep Warm Ocean
- Sky Pillar — Deep Ocean
- Secret Garden — high above a Deep Lukewarm Ocean
- Wish Cave — Blooming Plateau
- Deoxys Meteor — Small End Islands
- Hoenn League — Steppe

## Sinnoh

- Spear Pillar — Cold Shrubland
- Temple of Sinnoh — reached with the Azure Flute and plate route
- Snowpoint Temple — Wintry Lowlands; Regigigas route
- Crescent Isle — Deep Ocean; Cresselia route
- Fullmoon and Newmoon routes — linked through the Moonlight Amulet and sleep sequence
- Flower Paradise — Shaymin route with the required starter party
- Manaphy Ruins — Deep Cold Ocean
- Lakes Verity, Acuity, and Valor — Cherry Grove, Glacial Chasm, and Arid Highlands
- Turnback Cave and Distortion World — Giratina route
- Stark Mountain — Nether Wastes edge; Heatran route
- Sinnoh League — Desert Oasis

## Other destinations

The Outskirt Stand sells Zygarde Cells in the Desert Oasis. Four sealed shrines hold the Treasures of Ruin until their matching stakes are removed. The Eternatus Cocoon appears in The End and opens with the required Galar Particles. Custom villages can contain Pokémon Centers, Poké Marts, and larger shopping centers.

## Maps and radars

Place the appropriate regional Cartography Table beside an unemployed villager to create a Map Guide. Gym signature items and Legendary Poké Radars produce target maps. Use a Nether or End table for those dimensions, and add a Battle Seeker when you need a farther destination.

### Locator categories

| Category            | Use                                                                                                             |
| ------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Progression locator | A checkpoint-specific recipe points to one regional Gym or league structure.                                    |
| Legendary radar     | A species or group recipe points to its documented structure; recipes and dimensions differ.                    |
| General Poké Radar  | Four Iron Ingots, four Redstone, and one Locator Chip produce the shared component used by later radar recipes. |
| Direct exploration  | Some story locations document a biome route or optional command and do not accept another structure's locator.  |

### Craft-to-map workflow

1. Confirm the exact target, region, and dimension.
2. Obtain every named ingredient from the current item or encounter page.
3. Craft the locator or radar and read its tooltip.
4. If the tooltip names a Cartography Table, use the correct table to produce the map.
5. Travel to the marker, then apply the target guide's biome and vertical-search instructions.

A successful result is a direction, not proof that the entrance is at the visible surface. Kyogre's temple is on the Deep Cold Ocean floor, Whirl Island is also in Deep Cold Ocean, and the Eon Duo's Secret Garden sits high above a Deep Lukewarm Ocean.

Ho-Oh is one verified example: its radar recipe uses one Flying Gem, two Wind Charges, one each of Clever, Muscle, Resist, Genius, and Swift Feather, plus one Poké Radar. Its tooltip targets Bell Tower in the Overworld through an Overworld Cartography Table with a 10,000-block search range. This recipe does not generalize to other structures.

### Failed locator checks

- If the item does nothing, confirm whether it must be used in a Cartography Table.
- If no structure is returned, verify the dimension and search range, move to a new area, and retry before recrafting.
- If the marker appears empty, confirm the biome and search vertically for ocean-floor, underground, floating, or shared-building targets.
- If a server differs, record the version and ask whether its datapacks, structure settings, or generated chunks differ from the documented profile.
- Do not delete chunks as a first recovery step.

In a test world or with operator approval, exact `/locate structure` commands can verify a named target. They are diagnostic tools, not the survival route, and an unsafe returned Y coordinate should never be used blindly.
