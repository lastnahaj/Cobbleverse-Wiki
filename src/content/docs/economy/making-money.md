The documented 1.7.42 profile awards **CobbleDollars** from configured NPC and wild-Pokémon battles. The balance appears in merchant screens rather than as an inventory stack. The data does not establish one fixed payout for every opponent, so budget from the live bank value.

## CobbleMerchants

- A basic merchant can be created by placing a Display Case beside an unemployed villager. It has broad stock but higher prices.
- Poké Marts in custom villages offer a smaller selection at better prices.
- Large village shopping centers carry the widest selection, including TMs and Rare Candies, and generally have the best base-pack prices.
- The merchant Bank can buy supported items such as emeralds, Relic Coins, and Vitamins.

## Department-store families

Generated offers are organized into Farmers Market, regular Store Workers, randomized Store Workers, Relic Store Workers, and randomized Relic Store Workers. Specialist merchants include apricorn and seed sellers, the Master of Stones, TM Archivist, battle-item and held-item sellers, vitamins, food, and technology.

A randomized result belongs to a rotating pool; it does not mean one permanent worker always carries the offer. Prefer a fixed store result when one exists. If an item appears only in a randomized family, check another appropriate store or the server's documented refresh behavior.

Use the [Shop Browser](/battle/shops/) before traveling. It identifies the item, price, merchant, and store family. Search by the exact displayed item name when a recipe ingredient is unclear.

## Purchase priorities

- **Early:** preserve the starting supply. Spend only to restore the next attempt, replace exhausted capture resources, or complete the active locator recipe.
- **Mid-game:** invest in reusable team infrastructure such as needed TMs, held items, form requirements, and recipe materials after checking the selected Pokémon can use them.
- **Late-game:** reserve expensive specialist materials for a confirmed legendary, form, or regional objective.

At the documented 2026-07-30 snapshot, the Master of Stones listed type gems at 25,000 CobbleDollars each. Three gems would therefore cost 75,000. This is a budgeting example, not a permanent price promise; re-check the live catalogue before buying.

## Server markets

The Infinite Gaming Servers world also has Bazaar for fixed-price Pokémon listings and an Auction House for item bidding. Fees, limits, channels, and menu aliases are server configuration, so check the live interface before committing a valuable listing.
