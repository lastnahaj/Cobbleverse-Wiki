**Bazaar** combines a fixed-price Pokémon market with an item Auction House.

## Trading reference

- Pokémon listings use a set price and move completed or expired entries into a collection flow.
- Item auctions can record bid history and extend near-closing auctions to reduce last-second sniping.
- Auction channels can separate item categories or rule sets.
- Market history and statistics may be available through configured interfaces.

Open the live server menu for the exact entry point. Menu aliases, listing fees, durations, limits, tax, and channel names are controlled by Cobbleverse.

## Safe trading guidance

- Confirm item details before completing a player trade.
- Use only official server interfaces and staff guidance.
- Report suspicious behavior through the official community channels.

## Before listing

- Read the fee and final payout before confirming.
- Check Pokémon form, level, IVs, held item, and other visible details.
- Collect completed sales and returned entries from the collection box.
- Use only the official interface; direct trades do not inherit Bazaar protections.
