## Featured forms

| Form                    | Requirements                                                                                                                                                                |
| ----------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Ash-Greninja            | Find Ash's Cap at Ash's House, reach 255 friendship, use the cap on Greninja, then trigger Battle Bond by causing a knockout with a damaging move. It reverts after battle. |
| Ash's Pikachu           | Place Rage Candy in Pikachu's cosmetic inventory. Pikashunium Z enables its special Z-Move; its base stats do not change.                                                   |
| Ultra Necrozma          | Equip a Z-Ring, charge the Solarizer or Lunarizer with Solgaleo or Lunala, fuse it with Necrozma, then provide Ultranecrozium Z.                                            |
| Zygarde 10%             | Reassemble 9 Cells and 1 Core; processing takes about 2 minutes.                                                                                                            |
| Zygarde 50%             | Reassemble 49 Cells and 1 Core; processing takes about 5 minutes.                                                                                                           |
| Power Construct Zygarde | Reassemble 95 Cells and 5 Cores; processing takes about 10 minutes. Complete Form appears in battle below half health.                                                      |
| Mega Zygarde            | Use a Power Construct Zygarde holding Zygardite, wear a Mega Bracelet, reach Complete Form in battle, then Mega Evolve.                                                     |
| Eternal Floette         | Craft Floettite, wear a Mega Bracelet, and reach level 50. Light of Ruin must be restored after reverting from Mega form.                                                   |

## Quest-item families

- **Creation trio:** Adamant Crystal, Lustrous Globe, Griseous Core, Red Chain, Temporal Flute, Spatial Flute, and Azure Flute control Origin forms and the Arceus route.
- **Weather trio:** Blue Orb and Red Orb enable Primal forms; the Sky Core summons Rayquaza.
- **Rocket routes:** Ancient DNA, Cloning Catalyst, Synthetic Matrix, Rocket Armor, Corrupted Shard, and Shadow Heart support Mewtwo and Shadow Lugia paths.
- **Lake trio:** Mesprit Plume, Uxie Claw, Azelf Fang, and their three jewels build the repaired Red Chain.
- **Crown routes:** Calyrex Crown, Iceroot Carrot, and Shaderoot Carrot support Calyrex, Glastrier, and Spectrier.
- **Form tools:** Deoxys Meteorite, Prison Bottle, Reveal Glass, Rusted Sword, Rusted Shield, and Ogerpon's three masks change or unlock special forms.
- **Legendary locators:** Locator Chip, regional Poké Radars, proofs, keycards, journals, and cards point Cartography Tables toward quest structures.
- **Regi materials:** Dragon, Electron, Ice, Rock, and Steel ores contribute to the relics used at Regi structures.

Use REI to confirm current recipes. The [item catalogue](/Cobbleverse-Wiki/items/) covers indexed materials and held items; the quest-item families above connect the special components to their [encounter routes](/Cobbleverse-Wiki/progression/legendary-mythical/).
