Special encounters in the public base pack are often tied to the regional trainer series. Finish the required champion route before searching for later-region structures.

## Kanto routes

| Encounter                 | Route                                                                                                                     |
| ------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Articuno, Zapdos, Moltres | Explore their Snowy Plains, Stony Shore, and Nether Wastes towers and use the tower feather.                              |
| Mew                       | Defeat Blue, craft the Origin Fossil, and use the Jungle temple altar.                                                    |
| Mewtwo                    | Combine Ancient DNA with the Cloning Catalyst from Team Rocket's Kanto route; Armored Mewtwo adds Rocket Armor materials. |

## Johto routes

| Encounter              | Route                                                                             |
| ---------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Raikou, Entei, Suicune | Progress through the Burned Tower route after Kanto.                              |
| Ho-Oh                  | Obtain the Rainbow Wing from the Burned Tower path and take it to Bell Tower.     |
| Lugia                  | Obtain the Silver Wing through the Rocket Radio Tower route and use Whirl Island. |
| Celebi                 | Follow the Ilex Shrine route after the required champion progression.             |

## Hoenn routes

- Regice, Regirock, and Registeel use their regional temples and crafted relics; Regigigas later uses the Titan Relic.
- Kyogre uses the Ocean Core at its Deep Cold Ocean temple; Groudon uses the Earth Core at the volcano.
- Rayquaza's Sky Pillar route builds the Sky Core from the Aether Gem, Desolate Gem, Primordial Gem, and Emerald Emblem.
- Jirachi is tied to Wish Cave and the Melodic Tape. Deoxys uses a Meteorite Crystal at the End meteor structure.
- Defeating Hoenn Champion Rocco supplies Ruby Dew and Sapphire Dew for Latias and Latios.

## Sinnoh routes

- Lake Verity, Acuity, and Valor provide the Mesprit Plume, Uxie Claw, and Azelf Fang plus the jewels needed to repair the Red Chain.
- Dialga and Palkia use the Red Chain route; their Temporal and Spatial Flutes contribute to the Azure Flute.
- Arceus requires the Temple of Sinnoh route, the Azure Flute, and the plate collection.
- Giratina is reached through Turnback Cave and the Distortion World. Cresselia leads into the Moonlight Amulet and Darkrai route.
- Shaymin uses Professor Oak's letter after meeting the required starter-party condition. Heatran uses the Stark Mountain chain that produces the Magma Stone.

## Later and special routes

The base pack also includes the four Treasures of Ruin, each released after removing eight matching stakes; a Split Decision Temple for Regieleki and Regidrago; Crown Cemetery and Crown Spire routes for Calyrex and its steeds; and an End cocoon that requires 500 Galar Particles to release Eternatus.

## Finding the structures

Craft a target-specific Poké Radar with a Locator Chip, combine it with an empty map in the correct regional or dimensional Cartography Table, then follow the map or its tooltip coordinates. Locator Chips can appear in Ominous Vaults, End Cities, and Nether Bastions. Use REI for the current recipes.
