LumyMon is the public base pack's navigation and encounter layer. It connects regional progression to custom workstations, maps, altars, ores, and quality-of-life controls.

## Navigation tools

- Regional Cartography Tables turn Gym signature items and champion items into destination maps.
- Regional Poké Radars combine a Locator Chip with a target recipe to locate legendary structures.
- Nether and End destinations require the matching dimension's Cartography Table.
- A Battle Seeker can widen the search to a different copy of a structure.
- Apricorn Boxes provide portable storage for related materials.

## Altars and encounters

Custom altars cover many Generation 1–4 encounters plus later targets such as Eternatus, Regieleki, and Regidrago. Each altar expects a specific quest item. Encounters can vary in level, IVs, and shiny result, so an altar does not guarantee a particular roll.

LumyMon also supplies custom Arceus- and Darkrai-related dimensions and their travel mechanics.

## Quality-of-life controls

Remote PC is normally bound to **P** and the default quick dismount key is **N**. Riding Fire-types can protect against heat and lava, while riding Water-types can provide underwater breathing. Exact keybinds can be changed in Controls.
