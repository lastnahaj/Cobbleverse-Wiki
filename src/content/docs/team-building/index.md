## Start with a job

Build for a specific task: the next Trainer Card checkpoint, a structure route, capture utility, or general exploration. Give every slot a primary job such as physical damage, special damage, fast cleanup, defensive switching, recovery, status, support, or catching.

Type coverage is a diagnostic, not a score. A team can hit many types super effectively and still fail because every attacker is slow, fragile, or dependent on the same damage category.

## Balance the roster

- **Damage:** compare Attack and Special Attack before assigning moves. Keep credible physical and special routes when the matchup rewards them.
- **Speed:** identify the member that acts early and the one that can finish weakened targets.
- **Bulk:** decide which member safely enters against each repeated enemy type.
- **Recovery:** distinguish repeatable recovery from one-use items and passive healing.
- **Utility:** add sleep, paralysis, screens, hazards, removal, or capture support only when the objective benefits.
- **Reliability:** account for accuracy, setup turns, immunities, and items such as Focus Sash or Rocky Helmet.

Use only forms, moves, abilities, and held items that are available in the current world. A catalogue entry proves that data exists; it does not prove the required TM, tutor, item, or form trigger has been obtained.

## Validation limits

A planning tool should flag EV totals above 510, any individual EV above 252, and levels outside 1–100. Duplicate species may be a format warning rather than a universal Cobbleverse rule. Availability warnings should be checked against the selected form and the live server.

The browser Pokédex and team notes do not change the Minecraft save. Keep a separate backup of anything that matters outside the current browser.

## Brock example

The published Brock roster is Geodude level 16, Bonsly level 16 with Focus Sash, Cranidos level 18, and Onix level 20 with Rocky Helmet. The planning cap is 25, but the in-game Trainer Card remains authoritative.

Three useful roles to test are:

| Candidate | Proposed job                     | Reason                                                                 |
| --------- | -------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Squirtle  | Bulky Water damage               | Targets Rock/Ground members while providing an early defensive option. |
| Bulbasaur | Grass special damage and utility | Adds another Rock/Ground route with room for status or recovery.       |
| Mankey    | Physical Fighting pressure       | Avoids depending only on special damage.                               |

Set actual levels and learned moves, leave held-item slots empty until the item is obtained, and re-check the plan after every move, form, or ability change.
